        Match payments

        Paste your wallet's transaction history: a CSV export, copied memos, or z_listreceivedbyaddress output. Lines are matched by the invoice ID in the memo, or by an invoice's unique amount when the memo is missing. Nothing is uploaded.

        Automatic detection would need a viewing key and a lightwalletd connection; see the README roadmap.
